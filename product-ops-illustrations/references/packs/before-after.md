# Before and After

Use for two supplied states, a documented change, or an explicitly hypothetical comparison. Give both sides equal semantic scope and comparable scale. Preserve the same inputs and objectives unless their change is part of the brief. An attractive after state is not evidence of measured improvement.

**Fixed:** Shared identity, matched states, clear changed relationship, supported benefits only, purposeful human character.

**Adaptable:** Side-by-side loose groups, one continuous scene with a transition, object arrangement, and a contrasting operator action. Avoid rigid slide panels and red-versus-green success coding. Label the actual change rather than relying solely on "before" and "after".

**Failure check:** Does the after side remove a required human check, imply automatic control, or invent savings? If so, correct the comparison.
