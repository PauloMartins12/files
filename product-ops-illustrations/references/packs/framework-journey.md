# Framework and Journey

Use for value-chain stages, layers, responsibility groups, product stages, and decision journeys. Make it clear whether proximity means category, sequence, or dependency; do not add causal arrows to a framework that only groups ideas.

**Fixed:** Shared identity, understandable grouping or progression, one takeaway, purposeful human character, exact source vocabulary.

**Adaptable:** Loose shelves, stepping stones, overlapping work areas, nested spaces, a curved path, or a small set of layers. Do not default to a corporate pyramid or dense architecture chart.

**Failure check:** Could a layer be mistaken for a physical process, a product stage for a proven outcome, or a roadmap for completed delivery? Adjust labels and relationships.
