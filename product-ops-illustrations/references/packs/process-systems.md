# Process and Systems

Use for sequences, dependencies, bottlenecks, handoffs, material routes, and data routes. Preserve node order and all source-specified edges; omit nonessential internal detail. Use 3–5 main anchors when the source permits simplification, but never remove a critical checkpoint to hit a count.

**Fixed:** Shared identity, readable direction, supported relationships, one takeaway, purposeful human character, generous whitespace.

**Adaptable:** Horizontal or curved arrangement, object metaphor, number of anchors, arrow route, and the character's task and outfit. Keep loops and branches only when supported. Distinguish materials and information when both exist.

**Failure check:** Could a reader infer the wrong order, an invented connection, or a bypassed human gate? If yes, revise structure before styling.

Keep substantial whitespace and neat hand lettering. No extra title, metrics, connections, equipment, realistic dashboard, or decorative mascot.
