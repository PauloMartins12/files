# Mining asset library

Use these original transparent PNG cutouts when a brief names the corresponding part of the mining value chain. Inspect the actual asset before generation. The user-supplied examples are visual references, not evidence of a specific site's equipment or processing route.

All seven assets have actual transparent pixels outside the subject. Keep the cutout transparent in reusable files. A finished workflow illustration may place them on the skill's white canvas. Do not add the reference examples' sky, ground, roads, vegetation, horizon, shadows, text, or branding. Do not flatten a cutout onto white and label it transparent.

The asset silhouette and colors should remain recognizable when scaled into a diagram: terraced pit, conveyors and platforms at plant, ribbed loaded ore wagons, bulk ship. Reduce hatch detail at small sizes while preserving the structure; do not replace them with a blank warehouse, anonymous box train, or generic boat. Keep mining equipment more detailed than the diagram notation. The Realist and Cartoon variants share these equipment cues, while human rendering follows its selected version.

The cutouts are conceptual. The plant example does not establish crushing, screening, beneficiation, or any other specific sequence. A shiploader, truck, stockpile, or loaded wagon appears only when supported by the brief. Avoid invented site names, OEM marks, fleet models, tonnages, and safety or engineering claims.
