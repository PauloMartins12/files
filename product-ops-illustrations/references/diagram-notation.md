# Hand-drawn diagram notation

Use an Excalidraw-style visual language for arrows, flowchart shapes, records, data icons, stage connectors, and legends in both Realist and Cartoon diagrams. The nine editable [SVG primitives](../assets/diagram/material-arrow.svg) are starting references, not fixed layouts. Use slight contour wobble or double strokes, rounded pen ends, sparse short hatching, open arrowheads, and consistent human-scale line weight. The diagram should remain easy to scan at slide size.

| Meaning | Treatment | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Physical material | Solid amber hand-drawn line and open arrowhead | [Material arrow](../assets/diagram/material-arrow.svg) |
| Information or data | Dashed teal hand-drawn line and open arrowhead | [Information arrow](../assets/diagram/information-arrow.svg) |
| Neutral sequence | Charcoal hand-drawn line and open arrowhead | [Sequence arrow](../assets/diagram/sequence-arrow.svg) |
| Framework adjacency | Thin charcoal dotted connector without an arrowhead | Mining stage journeys |
| Process step | Loose rectangular outline, light fill or hatching | [Process box](../assets/diagram/process-box.svg) |
| Decision | Loose diamond; label the actor and outcome if needed | [Decision diamond](../assets/diagram/decision-diamond.svg) |

Additional editable examples: [document](../assets/diagram/document.svg), [database](../assets/diagram/database.svg), [human review](../assets/diagram/human-review.svg), and [handoff](../assets/diagram/handoff.svg). Keep icons simple, consistent, and subordinate to any detailed mining asset. In mixed mining/digital diagrams, the line style must reinforce the color so meaning does not depend on color alone.

Do not turn stage adjacency into an asserted material transfer. Do not attach an information arrow to a train or machine as an automatic control action unless supplied. Maintain clear endpoints, direction, labels, and human review gates. Avoid perfect vector geometry, thick solid block arrows, glossy UI cards, decorative gradients, and dense hatching that obscures a label.
