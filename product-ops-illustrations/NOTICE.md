# Provenance

Product & Operations Illustrations is a personal English adaptation of the illustration-workflow idea in [Ian Xiaohei Illustrations by Ian](https://github.com/helloianneo/ian-xiaohei-illustrations).

The mining and AI/BI guidance, named character and selectable wardrobe guidance, template recipes, and both Realist and Cartoon calibration sets were developed for this skill. Calibration briefs are fictional demonstrations, not statements about a real mine, company, product, or measured result. The default and alternate character sheets and all calibration illustrations use original compositions generated with the built-in image-generation tool, guided by the user's supplied references. No upstream character or example image is included.

## User-supplied references

The four photos in `assets/references/photos/`, four cartoons in `assets/references/cartoons/`, and three mining examples in `assets/references/mining/` were supplied by the user. Originals are preserved without removing their existing attribution. The cartoons visibly credit Tom Fishburne / Marketoonist. The photos provide wardrobe and appearance references, including the Rio Tinto jacket. These references, depicted trademarks, and any underlying rights remain with their respective owners; the upstream MIT license does not license them. The user has confirmed distribution rights for internal sharing of this package; this note does not assert rights for public redistribution.

Generated scenes use the photos for clothing fidelity, the cartoons for broad editorial illustration qualities, and the mining examples for equipment shape, industrial sketch detail, and color. The isolated mining PNG assets and hand-drawn notation SVGs are original derivative reference work created for this personal skill. Their dialogue, panels, signatures, and watermarks are not incorporated into the new illustrations.
